# Brag plan: Cedar Lane AI receptionist

**What it is:** an AI receptionist for a one-bay auto detailing shop that answers the phone (and chat, and browser voice) 24/7 and books straight into Google Calendar.
**For:** small service businesses that miss calls after hours or while the owner is under a car.
**What sets it apart:** it doesn't take a message, it finishes the job: checks real availability, books, logs the call, and only escalates what a human should handle.
**Most impressive claim:** ~1.2s from the caller finishing to the agent replying (measured on Vapi).
**Visual hook:** a phone ringing in a dark, closed shop at 11:47 PM, and something still answering.
**Real material shown:** the real call transcript from a recorded Vapi booking call, the real seed calendar (Mike Chen Thu 2:30-4:30 makes 3:30 full), the real Calls-sheet columns, the site's real footage, colors (#0b0c0e / #ff4d1a) and fonts (Barlow Condensed / Barlow).
**Tone:** `cinematic`, restrained: trailer-scale type, dips through black, no jokes.
**Share caption:** "The phone rang at 11:47 PM. Our AI receptionist checked the calendar, booked the 4:30, and logged the call."

## Angle
Missed calls are missed bookings. The video follows one real call from ring to calendar to log, then shows the handoff rule so it reads as trustworthy, not reckless.
